## September 14 (Day 1) — Arriving in Alden

- In the evening, the party came to Alden and took one big room with one big bed at **The Golden Sheaf** inn. 8 sp
- At dinner (12 sp for four), they heard the talk of three missing carts. Old Bram walked out into the night
- At sleep time, Karin was on Punya's right and Wren on his left. Noel went to sleep apart — or so it seemed

## September 15 (Day 2) — Questions and an ambush

- **Morning**: Punya woke with his face in Noel's boobs (long rest complete). Her face was calm, as always
- At breakfast, the innkeeper named the three carts: the miller's cart (two weeks ago), Bram's cart (last week), Hale's cart (three days ago). All of them were coming home from Ravenport in the evening
- **The miller** (at the watermill): his son Tam (20) drove the first cart. He never came home with the money. No cart, no horse, no blood was found. He told them about the stone bridge and the dark place between the hills
- **Hale's farm** (south): his brother Rowan drove the third cart. A broken lantern and wheel marks going off the road, then stopping. Hale's wife gave the lantern to Wren
- **Bram's farm** (east): his grandson Will drove the second cart. The horses came home alone at midnight, the harness straps cut clean. Wren cast **Speak with Animals** on the old mare: a sweet smell, a far song, blue lights, and Will shouting one time
- The promises began here: Punya promised Wren "later". Karin said it was not fair and asked for hers too. Noel said, "I heard it. I will remember it." (This is the start of the List)
- In the afternoon, Meg rented them a cart and her old horse Anvil (8 sp), and Bram lent his young horse Pip for free. They rode east, looked at the road, and waited at the shepherd's hut
- **The evening ambush**: Noel cast Mage Armor. Punya drove the cart in a hood, and the three girls hid under the cloth. Between the hills: a sweet smell, a soft song, blue lights. All four passed their Wisdom saving throws and did not sleep
- **The fight (four pixies)**: poison arrows put Punya to sleep for a short time. Noel's Sleep spell dropped two pixies. Karin killed one. One ran away. XP: 50 each (50 / 300 for all)
- They questioned the two prisoners (Tansy and Sorrel): the men are alive and sleeping under the standing stones; the Stone Hands (two giants) carry things; the door opens only at night; the black box with the grey seal sleeps by the Lady's bed
- **The run home (night)**: they raced the cart back to Alden. Behind them, the door opened, and one of the Stone Hands picked up the dead pixie very gently
- Dinner at the inn. Bram heard that Will is alive and sleeping (Bram cried like a child)
- The pixies slept in a lidded basket in the corner of the room. Wren fed them honey
- **Night**: Punya said "No sex tonight. A long rest." Karin wrote "Tomorrow" on her hand. Noel said, "Tomorrow night, then. I keep the list."

## September 16 (Day 3) — The deal and the night of promises

- **Morning**: Punya woke in Noel's boobs again (long rest complete; all Hit Points and Spell Slots back)
- At breakfast, the miller came and asked, "Is my Tam sleeping too?" Punya told him: "We will save them."
- More questions for the pixies: their "lights" burn in little glass cups in a dark room under the hill, and the cups are the Lady's chain on them. Lira said "no" one time and lost her light. The two prisoners have a limit: if they are not home tonight, their fires go out. They also learned the Lady's looks, her place, and her strength (built on the Green Hag: HP 82 / AC 17)
- **The big decision**: the party is too weak to go in now. They will grow strong first, then come back. The sleeping men do not grow old and do not suffer, so the rescue can wait
- They gave the cart and Anvil back to Meg (one more day: 8 sp. Purse: 44 gp 4 sp) and Pip back to Bram
- **The deal with the pixies** (Wren's try to scare them failed, and Tansy offered a trade): the pixies promised — tell no one about the party, guide them truly when they return, and never sing against them. The party promised — come back, steal Tansy's and Sorrel's fire-cups and Lira's empty cup, and set them free
- **The return signal**: at night, at the flat patch south of the road, Punya calls out the password: "Chinpyoro-supōn!" The answer is a light blinking three times — a pause — three times. Only one pixie comes (two means a trap). They do not use honey for the signal, because other pixies might find it
- In the evening, they freed the two pixies at the edge of the forest. Tansy's last words: "Chinpyoro... supōn. We will not forget." Far away, a light blinked: three times — a pause — three times
- **Night (the promises kept)**: Noel's List said Wren, Noel, Karin. Punya kept his word to all three (Wren → Noel → Karin). Noel drew one clean line through the list. The four of them slept close together [Sex: Wren][Sex: Noel][Sex: Karin]

## September 17 (Day 4) — To Ravenport

- **Morning**: Punya woke in Noel's boobs, as always (long rest complete)
- They paid the inn (third night: room 8 sp + dinner 12 sp = 2 gp. Purse: 42 gp 4 sp) and walked east
- They passed the place between the hills in the daylight. The standing stones on the north hill were quiet. In the shade on the south side, a small grey shape was watching — **Lira** (Punya's Perception saw her). Then she was gone
- In the evening, they came to **Ravenport**. Noel's plan: no visit to the Contact tonight. First, an inn, and listen to the town
